## What we found on the live site (unlocklab.ai)
- **Stale events.** The Events page sorts events into upcoming and past by comparing their dates with *build time*, because the pages are prerendered (`app/events/page.tsx`). Crawlers still see the Dec 3 and Dec 10, 2025 workshops as "upcoming," with "Registration details coming soon!" In browsers, the "past" view shows those events as empty Luma iframes, because they have no `embedUrl`.
- **Footer year.** The footer © year is frozen at build time, so it shows 2025.
- **Possible wrong date.** The "Building AI skills that scale" event is dated 2025-01-30 but was added in Oct 2025. It may have been meant as 2026-01-30. Unconfirmed.
- **Shared metadata.** Every page uses the same title and description (`app/layout.tsx`).
- **Missing crawl files.** There's no `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` or structured data.
- **Off-site actions.** "Join the Community" and "Teach a Class" link to untitled Notion pages. No location is stated anywhere.
- **Hosts blurb.** It describes Fidelic as "pioneering AI consciousness technology." Flagged for Rafael to review.

## Decisions Rafael has made
- **Main offer.** UnlockLab now centers on the course **"Claude Code and Cowork for Professionals"**, using the overview at `SkWx Command Center/Clients/Unlock Lab/Proposals/claude-command-center-build-overview-v2-2026-10-12.md`. Course details:
  - four live 2-hour sessions plus a 1:1 discovery call
  - $1,500 per person
  - taught by SkWx
- **Audience.** Mid-career professionals, 35–55, who want AI's benefits fast and don't have time to learn it the slow way. They should feel they've found a solution and want to have a conversation.
- **How people arrive.** Mostly by referral. Search and AI-agent findability matter too.
- **Main action.** A short form, not a booking calendar or direct email.
- **Cohort status.** The first cohort is forming now and dates depend on who signs up. Show no dates.
- **Branding.** Focus on SkWx as the team, not Rafael personally.
- **Structure.** Option B: a homepage that features the course, plus a separate `/course` page with the full details and the form.
- **Visual direction.** B, Editorial / executive: warm off-white paper, serif headlines (Fraunces in the mockup), clean sans-serif body text (Inter), SkWx violet (~#5b4bd1) as the only accent, generous spacing, no stock photos. Rafael didn't like the current colors and design.
- **Events.** Drop Events from the main navigation and keep a small "past workshops" section. Bring it back when events resume.
- **Form fields.** Rafael wants to know AI experience, industry, position, and specific problems they want to solve.
